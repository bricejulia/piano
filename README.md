# 🎹 Piano

A tiny, colorful piano that runs in the browser.

This is a toy project I built with my daughter, who started learning music this year. One octave, one color per note, and every note's name written on its tile, so she can find *do ré mi fa sol la si do* by herself.

## Play

Open `index.html` in any modern browser. Nothing to install, no build step, no audio files: every sound is generated by the browser.

## What it does

- **8 tiles**, from *do* to the next *do*. Click or tap them, or use the keyboard.
- **4 instruments**: piano, guitare, flûte and batterie (each tile becomes a drum).
- **Metronome** with adjustable tempo and 2/4, 3/4, 4/4 or 6/4 time.
- **Demo mode**: pick a song and watch the tiles light up as it plays.
  - Vive le vent
  - Au clair de la lune
  - Ah ! vous dirai-je, maman
  - Ode à la joie

## Controls

| Note | do | ré | mi | fa | sol | la | si | do |
|------|----|----|----|----|-----|----|----|----|
| Keys | A · 1 | S · 2 | D · 3 | F · 4 | G · 5 | H · 6 | J · 7 | K · 8 |

| Key | Action |
|-----|--------|
| Space | Start / stop the metronome |
| ↑ / ↓ | Tempo +5 / −5 (hold Shift for ±1) |
| Esc | Stop the demo |

## Add a song

Songs live in the `SONGS` list inside `index.html`. Each note is written as `name:beats`:

```js
{
  title: "My song",
  bpm: 100,
  notes: "do:1 re:1 mi:2 -:1 DO:2",
}
```

Use `do re mi fa sol la si` for the notes, `DO` for the high do, and `-` for a rest. Songs have to fit within one octave (do to DO).
